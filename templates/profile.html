Use Health Connect on your Android phone to push Samsung Galaxy Watch data to Cogniflow automatically. The webhook URL below is personal and token-protected — only devices using this exact URL can send data to your account.

Setup Instructions
  1. Install Health Connect on your Android phone from the Google Play Store (it may already be pre-installed on Samsung devices).
  2. Open Health Connect → App permissions → make sure Samsung Health is allowed to write Sleep, Heart Rate, SpO2, and Steps.
  3. Install a webhook automation app such as HTTP Shortcuts or Tasker on your phone.
  4. Create an automation that fires every morning (e.g. at 8:00 AM) and sends a POST request to the webhook URL above with your Health Connect sleep/HR/SpO2/steps data in JSON format.
  5. Alternatively, use Health Auto Export (iOS/Android): go to Automations → REST API, set the URL above, method POST, format JSON, and enable Sleep Analysis, Heart Rate, SpO2, and Steps.
  6. Trigger a manual sync — your data will appear on your dashboard within seconds (processing happens in the background so the app does not time out).
Security: your token is secret. Anyone with this URL can upload data to your account. Regenerate it if you believe it has been leaked.
